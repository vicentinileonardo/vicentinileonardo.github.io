## Overview

- A web app that allows users to register, login, and find other users based on their interests and location.
- A simple **chat system** was implemented as well.
- The goal of the project was to learn to design and develop **RESTful APIs** using Node.js and Express.js.
- The project was developed in a team of 5 people and Scrum was adopted as an agile workflow methodology.
- Pair programming was also adopted as a development technique.

## My role

- Given my previous experience with web development, I co-led the initial design of the project.
- I was responsible for the implementation of some of the core RESTful endpoints
